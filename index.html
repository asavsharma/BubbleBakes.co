Our Story

What started in a home kitchen.

What started during a strange and uncertain time became something unexpectedly meaningful. During the COVID period, baking and experimenting with desserts became a way to spend time creatively — a small, calming corner of the day.

Over time, that experimenting turned into something genuinely enjoyable. The happiness people felt when they received and tasted a dessert became the biggest reason to keep going. A simple batch of brownies could quietly make someone's day a little better.

What began from a home kitchen slowly grew into a small bakery idea, built around one thing: making desserts that make people happy.

More than just dessert

Why Bubble Bakes exists

Desserts have a way of showing up in the moments that matter — birthdays, celebrations, little gifts, quiet family evenings, comfort after a long day, or simply treating yourself for no reason at all. Bubble Bakes exists to make those moments a little sweeter, one freshly baked batch at a time.
